# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step. The dashboard is generated by a Python script from `averageMarks.xlsx` into a self-contained `dashboard.html` that opens with a double click. Charts are hand-written SVG; no runtime dependencies, no CDN.

## Users

The primary user is a class teacher (предметник) who prepares a performance picture of their class for a parent meeting or a teachers' council. Secondary: the school administration and parents, who see the same screen projected in a meeting room.

The teacher also edits the source averages in Excel, so the XLSX workbook stays the working instrument for data entry.

## Product Purpose

Two representations of one calculation:

- `XLSX` — the working instrument. Live formulas, editable averages, printed for the teacher's own archive.
- `HTML dashboard` — the showpiece. Read from a projector at a meeting, or opened on a phone; everything legible at a distance, nothing overlapping, nothing to install.

Success means the teacher can open one file and show the class picture in under a minute, and can regenerate it after editing the averages with one command.

## Positioning

A school performance report as a readable dashboard rather than a spreadsheet printout: the same arithmetic, presented at presentation scale, with the meaning of every metric written next to the number.

## Operating Context

- Source: an averages export (per-student subject averages). `0.00` means the subject was never assessed and must render as absent, never as zero. The repository ships a synthetic stand-in, `web/fixtures/averages-sample.xlsx`, in place of a real export.
- Rounding: half-up at 0.5 (`4.50 → 5`, `4.49 → 4`), stated inside the report.
- Grades are on the Russian 5-point scale: 5 excellent, 4 good, 3 satisfactory, 2 unsatisfactory.
- The dashboard is shown on a classroom projector, often in a bright room, viewed from the back row.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The dashboard is regenerated from the XLSX by script; it is never hand-edited.
- Both a light and a dark theme must exist and be switchable; the dark theme must be a genuine dark surface, not a dark rectangle placed on a white page.
- Colour must not be the only carrier of meaning — every mark composition chart also carries a legend and numeric values.
- The metric definitions travel with the numbers: успеваемость (no `2`) and качество знаний (no `2` and no `3`) are spelled out wherever they appear, because they describe different populations and are routinely misread.
- Charts never overlap each other or the surrounding text at any supported width.
- Student names are personal data; the dashboard is for internal school use and carries no external links.
- The XLSX workbook keeps live formulas so a corrected average propagates through every sheet.

## Brand Commitments

- Russian-language interface throughout.
- Restrained, "quiet school" register: legible and serious, not a marketing page. No decorative effects, no invented praise.
- Mark colours follow the muted school palette: 5 — soft green, 4 — ochre, 3 — terracotta, 2 — muted red. Exact values are still open; the semantic mapping is fixed.

## Evidence on Hand

- `web/fixtures/averages-sample.xlsx` — synthetic stand-in for a class export: 28 students, 16 subjects, 445 marks, dated 09.11.2026. Generated by `web/scripts/make_fixture.py`; every name and value is fabricated so no real school data lives in the repository.
- `skills/class-performance-report/` — the in-project skill that builds the XLSX workbook, with the category rules, rounding rule and verification loop.
- No photography, logos, brand assets or testimonial material exists for this product. Nothing may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. One calculation, two surfaces. The XLSX and the dashboard must never disagree; both derive from the same source and the same rounding rule.
2. The metric names the metric. A number without its definition next to it is a defect.
3. Legible from the back row. A chart that reads only at arm's length has failed.
4. Absence is not zero. An unassessed subject renders as absent everywhere.
5. Show the reason, not just the verdict. The teacher needs the per-subject breakdown behind a class-level percentage.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Colour is never the sole signal; mark charts carry legends and numbers alongside colour.
- Text contrast meets WCAG AA in both themes.
- Type scales for projection: body text no smaller than 16px, key figures far larger.
- Keyboard-reachable theme switch with an accessible name and state.
